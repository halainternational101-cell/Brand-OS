## Recording tips

- Record in a quiet room with soft furnishings (a bedroom works well). Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
- Use the phone's voice memo app. Record each numbered section as its own file, so a slip only means redoing one section.
- Leave 2 seconds of silence at the start and end of each file.
- Speak like you're talking to one friend over tea, not reading a speech. It's fine to change words so they sound like you.
- Send me the files (any format is fine) and I'll time the animation to your voice.
